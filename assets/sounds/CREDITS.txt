Sound effects
=============

Both cues come from freesound.org and are released under Creative Commons Zero
1.0 (CC0) — a dedication to the public domain, which asks for nothing in
return. They are credited here anyway: CC0 waives the requirement, not the
courtesy, and someone who wants more sounds that fit these ought to be able to
find where they came from.

success.wav
    "Success" by kagateni — https://freesound.org/s/404358/
    Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal

error.wav
    "Failure/Wrong action" by gabrielaraujo — https://freesound.org/s/242503/
    Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal

Both files are edited rather than original, and are the same edits used in the
accessengine project they were taken from. Each was converted to mono, trimmed
of its leading and trailing silence, given a 5 ms fade in and a 30 ms fade out
so neither end clicks, and levelled to the same RMS loudness — a mean of
-22.2 dB. That last step is the one that matters most: as published, the
failure sound was around 11 dB quieter than the success one, so the sound
telling you something went wrong would have been the one you struggled to hear.
